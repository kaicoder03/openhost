## 2026-07-20 - O(N) Single-Pass Bucket-Sort Fragment Reassembly Optimization
**Learning:** The previous implementation of fragmented answer record reassembly walked the entire DNS resource records list inside a `SignedPacket` once for every single expected index. This resulted in O(N²) string-filtering overhead, which becomes a major scaling bottleneck for packets with many records (or in pathological cases approaching the `MAX_FRAGMENT_TOTAL` of 255).
**Action:** Replace multiple target-record-focused iterations with a single, linear O(N) scan over `packet.all_resource_records()`. By parsing the record labels and indexing directly into a fixed-size bucket array (up to 256 fragments), we sort the incoming packets in-place. We then validate and reassemble the fragments sequentially in memory, improving worst-case runtime complexity from quadratic to linear.
