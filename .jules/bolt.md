## 2026-07-20 - [O(N) Single-Pass Bucket-Sort Fragment Reassembly]
**Learning:** In the openhost signalling loop, dialers reassemble multi-fragment answer packets `_answer-<client-hash>-<idx>` of up to 255 fragments. Walking the resource records of a `SignedPacket` multiple times (O(N^2)) to query and collect individual fragments is highly inefficient and creates an architectural bottleneck under peak dial loads. Iterating over all resource records exactly once and bucket-sorting them into a fixed-size `Vec<Option<String>>` of size 256 solves this in linear O(N) time with minimal allocation.
**Action:** Always process DNS packet resource records in a single pass rather than nested iterations, utilizing a pre-sized array/vector bucket sort when order is determined by small bounded integer suffixes.
